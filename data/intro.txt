Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique to grant LLMs access to external data.
It works by retrieving relevant documents and passing them to the model as context.
LangChain is a popular framework for building these types of applications.
Vector databases like Chroma or FAISS are used to store and search through document embeddings.
Hallucinations are reduced because the model is forced to use the provided facts.
